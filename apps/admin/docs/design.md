# Design and content
A light operations workspace: ink text, warm white canvas, restrained purple actions, crisp rules, compact accessible tables. Navigation names the current task. Responsive cards and charts collapse naturally; long URLs wrap. Tables use labeled scroll regions. Visible focus, semantic headings, form labels and reduced-motion support are required.
Directory includes native/migrated and active/deleted/all filters, slug/title/destination search, explicit sorting and 25/50/100/200/500 page sizes. Summary shows total, active, deleted, native, migrated, recorded lifetime clicks, top five active links and five recently clicked links.
Link detail has immutable short URL, copy/open/PNG QR actions, destination/title editor, confirmed soft deletion and Overview/Audience/Traffic/Events analytics. Avoid claims of verified human visits; display Unknown metadata, estimated visitors, India timezone and best-effort collection explicitly. No fabricated commercial content or production data.
